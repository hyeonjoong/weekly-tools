# Response to Reviewers — SERENE (BELL-001), manuscript SLP-2026-0417

We thank the editor and the three reviewers for their careful reading. Reviewer comments are
reproduced in italics, followed by our response. Line numbers refer to the revised manuscript.

Reviewer 1

Comment 1-1: The sample size justification is unclear. Please state the assumed effect size and
the resulting power.
Response: We agree and have added the power calculation to the Statistical analysis section
(lines 36-38):
"Assuming a between-group difference of 3.0 points on the ISI with an SD of 4.5, 42 participants
per arm provide 80% power at a two-sided alpha of 0.05."

Comment 1-2: Please report the number of completers by arm rather than the pooled figure.
Response: Revised as suggested. The first paragraph of Results now reads: "Of the 84 randomised
participants, 78 completed the week-8 assessment, comprising 40 of 42 in the active arm and 38 of
42 in the sham arm (Table 2)." The corresponding counts are also given in the new Table 2.

Reviewer 2

Comment 2-1: The generalisability of the findings is not discussed.
Response: We have added a sentence to the limitations paragraph: "Because both clinics serve an
urban adult population, the findings may not generalise to older adults or to patients with
untreated sleep apnoea." We also cite two new references that bear on this point.

Comment 2-2: It is not clear how the sham device was matched to the active device.
Response: We have expanded the masking paragraph: "The sham unit was acoustically matched to the
active unit in loudness and spectral profile, and neither participants nor outcome assessors were
informed which unit they handled."

Reviewer 3

Comment 3-1: The slow-wave activity comparison is presented as if it were confirmatory.
Response: We have rewritten that sentence so that its exploratory status is explicit: "Slow-wave
activity in the first two NREM cycles was numerically higher in the active arm, but the
between-arm contrast was prespecified as exploratory and is reported without inferential testing."
No numbers were changed.

We have added two new references (Jung 2022; Oh 2023) in support of the revisions above.
